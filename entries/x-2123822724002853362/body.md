[@sucodeee](https://x.com/sucodeee/status/2123822724002853362) shared a set of card animations for SwiftUI and the web: a brushed-graphite wallet card that grows real-looking frost when you freeze it, lets you scrape the frost away with your finger, and crumbles into grain when you delete it. The button opens a draft in Hey Atu. In our run, Atu built the whole wallet as one web page: Freeze spreads needle-like frost in from the edges with a snowflake badge, the pointer scrapes a dark path through it while ice flakes fall, Details changes the digits to the full number and CVV, and Delete asks for a second click before the card dissolves from left to right and a silver card takes its place.

Atu's frost is a little brighter than the post's, and our version leaves out the step-by-step add-card form with its typed card number.
