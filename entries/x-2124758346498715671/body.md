Aesthetic Remake is our own series: each episode takes a project we liked and rebuilds it with one prompt. EP02 remakes [Pixel Play by FeralUI](https://feralui.dev/pixel-play), which we found via [@SolutionB2u](https://x.com/SolutionB2u/status/2119458988165370270): a cherry tree, a shrub and a maple, each with a skeleton, that shake when clicked, bend when dragged and sway when the weather turns on. Our remake keeps the core interactions; its trees come out a little smaller than the original's. The episode then spins it off into a row of wind chimes that ring in the same breeze.
