Aesthetic Remake is our own series: each episode takes a project we liked and rebuilds it with one prompt. In EP01 we remade [claude-photo-sorter by kairothq](https://github.com/kairothq/claude-photo-sorter), a viral open-source camera-roll sorter, for content creators. The app reads every clip in a footage folder on the Mac and files it into talking heads, outfits, B-roll, screen recordings and duplicates, while the thumbnails fly from a spinning 3D ball into category cards. In the episode, 141 clips are sorted live on a Mac.
