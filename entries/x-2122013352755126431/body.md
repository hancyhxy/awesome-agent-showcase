A small game that you play in your browser: a kid and a dog walk home through hand-painted scenes, with no timers and no losing. Atu draws the pictures and makes the sounds in code, builds the game in a new folder and opens it for you.

- The game in the video is Getting There by Rubzem: 14 chapters, made in four hours and polished since. Atu's first try is smaller, five calm scenes. Ask for a change at a time, such as a canoe ride or a train.
- The sound starts on your first click, because browsers need that.
