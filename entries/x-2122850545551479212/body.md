[@anikrisn](https://x.com/anikrisn/status/2122850545551479212) posted an eight-second pixel short: a tiny diver leaps into the dark, splashes down and swims up a ragged river whose surface changes pattern as the swimmer moves. The button opens a draft in Hey Atu. In our run, Atu built it as one canvas page at 192×108 with no libraries, layered like a 2D game: the run-up and dive, rings of dots, a front-crawl swimmer with a blue poster-paint wake, a river in perspective that cuts between moss, contour lines, squares, leaves, stars, zigzags, eye spots and tartan, and a push-in that loops. It also made a director's panel with sliders, colour pickers and a shot timeline to tune the look.

Our run used Claude Sonnet in Hey Atu. Our textures are generated by code, so they are tidier than the textures in the source animation, and details such as the panel's controls differ from run to run.
