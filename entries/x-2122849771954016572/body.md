[@s5style](https://x.com/s5style/status/2122849771954016572) shared a wedding invitation site where a four-point star changes shape as you scroll and carries you from one section to the next. The button opens a draft in Hey Atu. In our run, Atu built an invitation for an invented couple, Mira and Theo: the star framed Welcome, folded into the message card, wiped to About us and shrank into a dot before Our story.

Our version uses type, geometric shapes and a subtle grain texture, with no photos.
