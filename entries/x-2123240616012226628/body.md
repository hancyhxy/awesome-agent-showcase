[@fredremotion](https://x.com/fredremotion/status/2123240616012226628) posted a short sci-fi scan of a glowing sea creature that cuts with glitches between a honeycomb shell, a cyan jellyfish, an ASCII version and a violet wireframe, with small HUD labels on its parts. The button opens a draft in Hey Atu. In our run, Atu built it as one Three.js page from particles with bloom: a dark honeycomb sphere with glowing rims, a translucent blue jellyfish with glowing oral arms that flashes pink, an ASCII beat and violet shards tied by a network of white lines, each with pinned labels and a glitch cut, looping about every 8 seconds.

The labels and IDs are our own. Our honeycomb and jellyfish are dimmer and more see-through than the post's, and the camera orbits slowly instead of following the post's edit.
