# Third-party credits and rights

This repository shows other people's public work. It does not take ownership of that work.

## Rules

- Every item names its original source and credits the author.
- Our MIT licence and CC BY 4.0 licence cover only our own code, text and assets. They do not cover posts, media, names or marks of other people.
- A stored third-party asset needs a rights record in its item: `licensed` (the licence and its URL), `permission` (the holder and the date) or `quoted` (see below). Without one, the validator rejects the file and the item links to the source instead.
- Company promotional media has no default permission.
- An original redraw of third-party work is not automatically free of the original owner's rights. Check before you store one.
- The posts and tools in this collection are examples that the editors found useful. Inclusion is not an endorsement and does not mean the owner has approved it.

## Quoted excerpts

An `x-post` item may store a short excerpt of the media of its own source post. The record has the basis `quoted`, the author's handle, the link to the post and the part that we quote (for example `0:16-0:20 of the post video`).

- A recreation may quote the source author’s artwork in a short recording. Its caption names the artist and links to the original work. The collection does not include the artwork files or the page source.
- The excerpt is a GIF, WebP or MP4 of at most 8 seconds and at most 2 MB. The source post stays linked and the author stays credited ("via @handle").
- A quoted excerpt stays the author's work. Our licences do not cover it, and we do not sell it.
- To ask for removal, write to support@heyatu.com or open an issue. We remove the excerpt on request. We aim to remove the stored file first and fix the item after.

## Credits

The rights record of each item names the holder and the source of every stored third-party file.

This project has no third-party code. Its scripts use only Node built-in modules.

## Removal

If you own work that an item shows and you want a change or a removal, write to support@heyatu.com or open an issue. We aim to remove the stored asset first and fix the item after.
