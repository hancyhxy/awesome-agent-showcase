# Third-party credits and rights

This repository shows other people's public work. It does not take ownership of that work.

## Rules

- Every item names its original source and credits the author.
- Our MIT licence and CC BY 4.0 licence cover only our own code, text and assets. They do not cover posts, media, names or marks of other people.
- A stored third-party asset needs a rights record in its item: `licensed` (the licence and its URL) or `permission` (the holder and the date). Without one, the validator rejects the file and the item links to the source instead.
- Company promotional media has no default permission.
- An original redraw of third-party work is not automatically free of the original owner's rights. Check before you store one.
- The posts and tools in this collection are examples that the editors found useful. Inclusion is not an endorsement and does not mean the owner has approved it.

## Credits

No third-party work is included yet.

This project has no third-party code. Its scripts use only Node built-in modules.

## Removal

If you own work that an item shows and you want a change or a removal, open an issue. We aim to remove the stored asset first and fix the item after.
